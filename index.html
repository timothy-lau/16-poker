16 POKER

A memory game

How to play

  1. 16 cards are shown for the time you choose (10s, 20s, or Custom from 1 to 30s), then flipped face-down.
  2. The dealer turns 3 more cards face-up.
  3. From memory, tap 2 of the hidden cards to make the best 5-card poker hand with the dealer’s 3. Tap again to deselect.
  4. Every deal hides one best hand and a few tempting near-misses. Best = 100 pts, 2nd = 60, 3rd = 40, 4th = 25, 5th = 15, anything else = 5. Five rounds. Shorter times also bring closer decoys.
