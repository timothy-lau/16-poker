16 POKER

A memory game

How to play

  1. 16 cards are shown, then flipped face-down after a short countdown.
  2. The dealer turns 3 more cards face-up.
  3. From memory, tap 2 of the hidden cards to make the best 5-card poker hand with the dealer’s 3. Tap again to deselect.
  4. Score points for your hand, plus a bonus if you match the best possible combo. Five rounds.
